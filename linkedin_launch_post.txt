The data is already in Airtable. But you can't hand clients access to the whole base.

--- POSTING NOTES ---

- DO NOT put the link in the post body — LinkedIn suppresses reach by ~25-35% for posts with outbound links. Always put the link as the first comment instead.

- BEST TIME TO POST: Tuesday or Wednesday, 10am–12pm ET. Wednesday 10am is the single most-cited sweet spot across 2024–2025 research (Sprout Social, HubSpot, Buffer, Hootsuite all agree).

- Also good: Tuesday–Thursday 7–8am (pre-work) or 12–1pm (lunch scroll).

- For nonprofit audiences specifically: Thursday also performs well. Evening posts (6–8pm) occasionally outperform for cause-driven content since that audience scrolls outside work hours.

- Avoid: Friday after 2pm, weekends, Monday morning.

- Stay present for the first 60–90 minutes after posting. Reply to every comment — even briefly. Early engagement velocity is what tells LinkedIn's algorithm to keep distributing the post.

- Do not edit the post after publishing. Editing can reset the distribution clock.

- Do not post more than once that day.
