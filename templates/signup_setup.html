How to create your Airtable token
  1. Go to airtable.com/create/tokens
  2. Click Create new token
  3. Under Scopes, add:
    • data.records:read
    • data.records:write
    • schema.bases:read
  4. Under Access, add the specific base you want to connect
  5. Click Create token and copy it
